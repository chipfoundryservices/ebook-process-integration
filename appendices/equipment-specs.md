| Integration Metric | 3nm Logic (N3B / N3E) | 2nm GAA Logic (N2 / A16) |

| **Minimum Metal Pitch (M0/M1)** | $21	ext{–}24	ext{ nm}$ | $18	ext{–}20	ext{ nm}$ |
| **Power Delivery Grid** | Frontside M0-M15 | Backside Power Delivery (BSPDN) |
| **Wafer Thinning Depth** | N/A ($775\mu	ext{m}$ substrate) | Thin to $30	ext{–}50	ext{ nm}$ |
| **Total Litho Mask Count** | 75 to 85 Masks | 85 to 95 Masks |
| **EUV Exposure Layers** | 18 to 24 Layers | 25 to 30 Layers (High-NA on A14) |
| **Fab Cycle Time** | 100 to 115 Days | 110 to 130 Days |
| **Wafer Selling Price (ASP)** | **$18,000 – $22,000** | **$25,000 – $30,000+** |
