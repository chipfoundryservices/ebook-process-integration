# Chapter 7: Design-Technology Co-Optimization (DTCO) & Standard Cell Scaling

## 7.1 Beyond Geometric Pitch Scaling
Traditional Moore's Law relied on shrinking contacted poly pitch (CPP) and metal pitch (MP). At sub-3nm nodes, pitch scaling slows to $<10\%$ per node due to quantum limits. **Design-Technology Co-Optimization (DTCO)** achieves cell area scaling through layout and structural innovations:
- **Single Diffusion Break (SDB):** Eliminating double-dummy gate isolation between adjacent standard cells, reducing cell width by $10\text{–}15\%$.
- **Contact Over Active Gate (COAG):** Landing the gate via directly on top of the active gate channel rather than offsetting it over isolation dielectric, saving $10\%$ cell height.
- **Track Height Reduction:** Compressing standard logic cell heights from 6T (six metal tracks) to 5T, and finally to 4.5T/4.0T through Backside Power Delivery.
